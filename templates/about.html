Our Heritage

Chai Isn't Just A Drink — It's How We Live.

TEACHAR wasn't conceived in a corporate boardroom or born from a market spreadsheet. It was born in the quiet, fragrant dawn of a home kitchen where the kettle was always simmering, and the door was always open.

Our founder grew up in a home where every emotion had its own brew. Morning clarity came with strong Assam CTC and freshly bruised ginger root. Family reunions were celebrated over cardamom-infused Masala Chai. Long rainy afternoons were spent sipping piping hot Lemon Tea in hand-fired earthen Kulhads.

"We realized that in a fast world rushing toward instant powders and vending machines, the soul of authentic Chai was fading. So we built TEACHAR — a sanctuary for everyone who loves tea and lives with it."
